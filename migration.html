DATAPORT & INTEGRATION

Bring your business information with you.

Moving from spreadsheets and disconnected systems is often the hardest part of change. DataPort is intended to make import, export and reconciliation a first-class business process.

Understand the sourceMap and validateHandle errors without losing controlPreserve portabilityIntegrate selectively
01 / 05

Understand the source

Identify source applications, record ownership, duplicates, field meanings and retention requirements before transferring anything.

02 / 05

Map and validate

Use structured templates and mapping rules for customers, contacts, opportunities, accounting data and relevant transaction records. Validate required fields, relationships and business rules before acceptance.

03 / 05

Handle errors without losing control

Large imports need repeatable validation, explicit error reports, safe retries and clear reconciliation. A file upload is not a completed migration.

04 / 05

Preserve portability

Customers should have governed export options for their business records, subject to permission and applicable legal or contractual constraints.

05 / 05

Integrate selectively

Accounting, banking, productivity and other external integrations require explicit connector contracts, credentials, consent, monitoring and failure handling. Specific third-party integrations are not claimed as live on this site.

DATAPORT REQUIREMENTS

Every important record needs a route in and a route out.

DataPort Import / Export Studio is a platform capability requirement. These categories are in the intended scope; downloadable production templates, accepted mappings and verified import volumes will be released only after their data contracts pass qualification.

Commercial data

Customers, contacts, opportunities, activities, quotation references and the relationships between them.

Financial data

Chart of accounts, opening balances, invoices, bank accounts and transaction data with explicit reconciliation.

Operations data

Suppliers, stock records, projects, assignments, reference data and module-specific transactions where qualified.

What a qualified bulk-migration flow must demonstrate

Downloadable documented templates; import/export mapping; validation before commit; record-level error reports; safe and idempotent retries; reconciliation of counts and totals; role-based access; and portable exports. Claims for thousands of records require representative measured tests, not an illustrative screenshot.
