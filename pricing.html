COMMERCIAL TRANSPARENCY

What a serious proposal should make clear.

TURBINE is intended to be adopted as one BusinessOS. The commercial offer must still explain the actual responsibilities, licence boundaries and any separately scoped transition work.

Subscription scope

Included and excluded capabilities, number of authorised users or other ratified metric, service period and entitlement controls.

Adoption costs

When relevant: discovery, blueprint, mapping, validation, third-party connector work, training and rollout milestones.

Support and changes

Published support commitments when ratified, renewal treatment, data portability, cancellation and governing terms.

No pricing, currency commitments, instant-checkout capability or availability dates have been invented.
