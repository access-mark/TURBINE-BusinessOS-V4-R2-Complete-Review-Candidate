WHO TURBINE IS FOR

From sole proprietor to established enterprise

The same holistic operating principle applies to a one-person practice, a growing team and an established SME. The configuration and governance evolve with the business.

One BusinessOS, configured to the size of the business

We do not sell a different collection of apps to each customer. The complete BusinessOS is the operating environment. Discovery determines how it is configured, which workflows apply, how historical records migrate and which capabilities are qualified for the intended deployment.

Sole proprietors and freelancers

For sole proprietors and freelancers, full TURBINE adoption means bringing customer relationships, commercial commitments, delivery, purchasing, financial records and day-to-day decisions into one coherent environment. The configuration and delegated responsibilities change with the organisation’s scale, but the underlying operating principle does not.

One person is responsible for sales, delivery, administration and getting paid. TURBINE should reduce the number of places that person must check.
