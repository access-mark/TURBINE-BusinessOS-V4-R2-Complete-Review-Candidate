INDUSTRY SOLUTIONS

Built for the work behind your industry.

Different sectors share core commercial and financial needs, but their operational journeys differ. TURBINE is designed to adapt around those journeys, with suitability assessed during discovery.

Professional servicesIT and managed servicesManufacturingRetail, wholesale and distributionMedical and healthcare-adjacent servicesMining and industrial servicesAgencies and consultanciesProperty and professional practices
01 / 08

Professional services

Connect prospects, client engagements, quotations, time-sensitive deliverables, approvals, billing and outstanding balances. The operational question is simple: what was promised, who owns the work and what still needs to happen before payment?

02 / 08

IT and managed services

Coordinate customer relationships, recurring service commitments, equipment, projects, procurement, support work and commercial follow-up. Preserve context between account teams, technical delivery and finance.

03 / 08

Manufacturing

Connect customer demand, material requirements, purchasing, stock and operational execution. Manufacturing-specific planning, production and costing depth must be validated against the intended operating model.

04 / 08

Retail, wholesale and distribution

Understand customers, product availability, purchasing, receipt of goods, stock movements, invoicing and margins. Multi-location, point-of-sale and goods-only invoicing requirements must be explicitly assessed.

05 / 08

Medical and healthcare-adjacent services

Support governed commercial and administrative work, supplier purchasing, assets, scheduling and billing where appropriate. TURBINE is not being presented as a clinical records system or as certified for regulated clinical workflows.

06 / 08

Mining and industrial services

Connect service contracts, customer projects, procurement, field resources, equipment and financial oversight. Site safety, regulatory and specialist asset-management requirements need independent scope validation.

07 / 08

Agencies and consultancies

Follow opportunities into scoped work, project execution, change requests, invoicing and collections, with clearer accountability for deliverables and commercial commitments.

08 / 08

Property and professional practices

Bring clients, appointments, documentation, service requests and billing into a governed operational view. Industry-specific compliance and trust-accounting requirements require separate evaluation.
