SECURITY & GOVERNANCE

Trust is an operating requirement.

Business information is commercially sensitive. TURBINE’s intended control model puts identity, tenancy, permission boundaries, audit and recoverability at the centre of the platform.

Tenant isolationIdentity and least privilegeAudit and evidenceRecovery and resiliencePrivacy and data handlingQualification before production
01 / 06

Tenant isolation

Each customer organisation requires a demonstrably enforced isolation boundary. Isolation must be verified at database, API and service layers, including negative and concurrency tests.

02 / 06

Identity and least privilege

Authorised users should see only the records and actions their roles permit. Authentication, session controls, invitation and recovery flows must be independently qualified.

03 / 06

Audit and evidence

Important commercial and operational changes need attributable, durable audit records. Audit trails should help explain who did what and which record or approval was authoritative.

04 / 06

Recovery and resilience

Backup success alone is insufficient. Restores, application restart, recovered data access and operational recovery objectives require repeatable tests.

05 / 06

Privacy and data handling

South African privacy requirements, customer consent, data retention, export and deletion obligations must be addressed in approved policies and production contracts. This preview is not a security certification.

06 / 06

Qualification before production

Current programme work is subject to technical, financial and operational gates. This site intentionally avoids claims of completed certifications, production availability or specific security guarantees that have not been independently evidenced.

EVIDENCE BEFORE TRUST CLAIMS

What we will require before production access.

Security is not a badge. Relevant controls must be demonstrated through tests, retained evidence and operating procedures appropriate to the approved release scope.

Isolation proof

Negative tenant-crossing tests, role and session enforcement, API checks and tamper-resistant audit evidence.

Operational recovery

Backups, independent restores, recovered-browser checks and measured recovery objectives.

Commercial integrity

Entitlement decisions, billing state, finance auditability and event processing that can be reconciled.

Technical and commercial qualifications remain programme gates; this site does not claim completed certification.
